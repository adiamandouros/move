Privacy

Last updated: {{updated}}

In short: Wayhop has no accounts, uses no cookies or analytics, and doesn't track you.

What stays on your device

  • Your language (English or Greek), saved in your browser so pages open in it next time.
  • Copies of pages and timetables, saved by the app so it works offline.
  • Your location, if you allow it: your browser uses it to find the bus stops and metro station nearest to you. It isn't sent to Wayhop's server.

You can clear all of this in your browser's settings for this site.

What reaches our server

  • The bus stops you're looking at. To show live arrivals, the bus page asks the server for nearby stops by their number. Those stops are near you, so they show roughly where you are, but they aren't kept with anything that identifies you. Arrivals are kept per stop for a few minutes and shared by everyone.
  • Your IP address, for one minute. To share OASA's live data fairly, the server counts each address's requests for new stops over one minute, in memory only.
  • Standard web server logs. Like any website, our host's web server logs each request (IP address, page, time and browser) for security and to fix problems.

Wayhop's server, not your device, asks OASA for live data, and those requests carry only the stop number.

Hosting

Wayhop is hosted by Mediahost, on servers in Germany, in the European Union.

Other websites

Walking directions open Google Maps, and announcements link to the OASA and STASY websites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Wayhop keeps almost nothing that identifies you, but contact us with any question. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (opens in a new tab).

Changes

If anything here changes, for example if Wayhop starts showing ads, this page will say so first.
