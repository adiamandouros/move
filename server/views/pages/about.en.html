About Wayhop

Wayhop helps you get around Athens by public transport: live bus arrivals at the stops near you, metro trips that show which carriage to ride for the exit, service announcements, and practical information like closing times, tickets and night buses.

Free, and ready for the underground

Wayhop is free and needs no account. Once you've opened them, the subway planner and the info page also work without a connection.

Where the information comes from

  • Live bus arrivals: OASA's real-time service.
  • Timetables, lines and stops: OASA's open data (opens in a new tab), updated every week.
  • Service announcements: the OASA (opens in a new tab) and STASY (opens in a new tab) websites.
  • Which carriage to ride: collected for Wayhop. Where it isn't confirmed yet, the page says so.

Wayhop is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by OASA, STASY or OSY. Times and arrivals can be wrong or out of date, so when it matters, check the announcements at the station too.

Accessibility

Wayhop aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA: every page works with a keyboard and a screen reader, in English and in Greek. If something doesn't work for you, please let us know.
